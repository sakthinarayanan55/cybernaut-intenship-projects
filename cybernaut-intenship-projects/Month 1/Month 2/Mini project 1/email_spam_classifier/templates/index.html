Inference Pipeline Architecture

Zero data retention end-to-end classification workflow

01

Pre-Processing

Standardizes URLs, phone digits, currency symbols, and applies Porter Stemming to collapse inflections.

02

TF-IDF Vectorization

Maps raw tokens into a sparse high-dimensional n-gram vector with term-frequency inverse document penalization.

03

Support Vector Machine

Evaluates margin distance with Platt sigmoid calibration to yield calibrated probabilities and threat level indices.

04

Explainable Attribution

Performs real-time dot-product attribution to decompose predictions into human-readable keyword contributions.
